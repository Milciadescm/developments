Reversing the losing trades

Each trade the bot took was replayed on the same bars with the opposite side, in two ways. The mirror reverse takes the other side with the stop at the original target and the target at the original stop. The 1:1 reverse keeps the original stop distance. A reverse was adopted only if it beat the original by more than 0.05R on 2005–2015 data (micro: before June 2025), then judged on the later data it never saw.
