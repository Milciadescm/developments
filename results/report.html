Backtest report · XAUUSD · XAGUSD · BTCUSD · US500 · NAS100

Fib-FVG Dual Engine

A weekly swing engine (Monthly/Weekly bias, Daily Turtle breakout, entry in the 0.618–0.786 Fibonacci OTE zone, targets at the −0.272 and −0.618 extensions) plus a 4H FVG micro engine. Tested on real price history with broker lot rules and costs.

The short version
  • The edge is real but thin: +0.10R per weekly entry, +0.17R per Turtle add, +0.06R per 4H micro trade, after spread.
  • Over 2005–2026 at $50k with 0.5% per unit, it made +61% in total (2.2%/yr) with a 41% max drawdown. Profits came in bursts: 2008–09, 2013, 2017 and 2025.
  • US500, NAS100 and XAGUSD made the money. XAUUSD and BTCUSD lost money net on these rules.
  • Accounts under $1,000 cannot size most signals. Even 0.01 lot would risk too much, so the bot skips them instead of over-leveraging.
  • At 0.25% risk, a 6% trailing-loss prop rule is breached in every 10,000-trade simulation (median after ~376 trades). At 0.10% risk, the chance within 500 trades is 1.5%.

The billions question

What compounding can and cannot do

The math from your video is the engine here. The ratio of consecutive Fibonacci numbers (5/8, 8/13, 13/21…) converges to 0.618, which is the OTE entry and the extension targets. Fibonacci gives the levels. It does not create edge by itself. Edge is measured, and here it is about 0.1–0.2R per trade.

Growth per trade ≈ risk × avg R = 0.005 × 0.116 ≈ 0.058% $1,000 → $1,000,000,000 needs ln(1e6) = 13.8 log-units of growth Trades needed ≈ 13.8 / 0.00058 ≈ 23,827 trades ≈ 133 years at 180/yr

Raising risk to get there faster runs into drawdown first: at 3% per unit the median 10,000-trade path falls 99% at some point. Liquidity also caps size. Past a few million dollars, orders move the price, and the per-order lot limits in the backtest start to bind. A realistic goal for this system is steady growth at 0.5% risk, with the funded account as the main lever for scale.

Scope

What was and was not tested

  • Data: Yahoo Finance daily bars (gold and silver futures and S&P from 2000, BTC from 2014, Nasdaq-100 from 1985; trading from 2005) and hourly bars for the last ~2 years, built into 4H. Index hourly bars cover cash hours only. Futures stand in for spot XAU/XAG.
  • Not tested: the S&P 15s/30s/45s FVG idea. No second-level history was reachable from here. The EA has it as an experimental module, off by default, for demo forward testing only.
  • Fills: limit orders fill at the level or a better open. If a bar touches both stop and target, the stop counts first. Spread is charged on every trade. Swap and commission are not modelled.
  • Tuning: weekly rules were fixed before testing and stayed positive in both 2005–15 and 2016–26. The micro engine variant was picked from a 108-combination grid on data before June 2025. It held up afterwards, but only just (+0.03R before costs), so treat it as unproven.
  • Contract specs (contract size, 0.01 min lot, spread) are typical MT5 CFD values. Check them against FundedNext's symbol specifications before trusting the small-account rows.
