FibFVG Pro Playbook

Only the plans that made money on 2024+ data. The plan for instant accounts (no profit target, no consistency rule, 6% max loss), Normal vs Aggressive, and the 12-month payout roadmap tested from 16 different start months.

The short version
  • No fixed-2R intraday plan has an edge. Asia-range and previous-day sweeps with a 2R target lost money in both halves of the data, and so did a 2R target on the current entries. All the losing plans were removed from the code.
  • The plans that make money already pay more than 2 to 1: the average win is 2.2–2.5R against an average loss of 0.7R. That edge comes from letting winners run.
  • New plan, Core + 2R bank: takes half off at +2R and lets the rest run. The edge per trade is a little lower, but profit is closed sooner. The first reward came within 90 days in 69% of start months (44% without banking), and the worst 12-month result was +$3,827 instead of breakeven.
  • A first reward in month 1 happened in only about 1 start month in 5. Plan on months 2–3 for the first reward.
  • No breach in any plan, mode or start month. The guard keeps drawdown plus open stop-risk under 5% (the firm line is 6%).

Weak spots

What the trade logs say

  • Fixed 2R targets: on the levels entries +2R is hit before the stop only ~35% of the time, about breakeven after costs.
  • Hours: the result for each New York hour flips between the two halves of the data, so no hour is a reliable weak spot. Kill-zone-only entries were not better than all hours.
  • Weekdays: Thursday was slightly negative in both halves (−0.03R and −0.16R). It's too small to act on.
  • News: CPI days lost in both halves but only 14 trades fell on them, so skipping CPI made no measurable difference. FOMC and NFP days did well.
  • Markets: gold and silver carry the plans. BTC is roughly flat.

Before you buy

Check these with each firm

  • Minimum and maximum amount per reward, and how soon the first reward can be requested.
  • Maximum funded capital per trader, and whether the same bot may run on several accounts. Six $100K accounts at one firm may exceed its limit.
  • Whether a reward can be requested with trades open. The bot shows "withdrawable now" and adjusts its guard after each reward.
  • Profit split (80% assumed) and when the refundable fee is returned (assumed with the first reward).
  • Lucid Trading is a futures prop firm. Its accounts trade CME futures on NinjaTrader, Tradovate or Rithmic, not MT4/MT5/cTrader CFDs, and its drawdown is measured in dollars. This bot needs a futures version first. I don't have Lucid's current prices and rules, so its row here is a placeholder priced like Instant Premium.
  • All accounts take the same trades, so drawdowns arrive on every account at once. More accounts scale income and risk together.
