Our story

A family market built on fresh food and friendly service.

Saint Tropez Supermarket was founded to give our neighbourhood a market that feels like a summer morning on the coast: bright, fresh and welcoming. We work with local suppliers, bake every day and know many of our customers by name.

Whether you need a quick baguette or the full weekly shop, our team is here to help.
